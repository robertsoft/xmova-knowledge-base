# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

As maintainers and contributors, we pledge to respect all people who
contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating
documentation, submitting pull requests, and other activities.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting: roberto.ribeiro@simova.com.br

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the
code of conduct. Reports will be handled promptly and with respect.

For more information, see the full Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org
